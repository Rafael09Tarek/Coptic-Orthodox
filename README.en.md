# ✝️ Coptic Orthodox — English

## 🇬🇧 English

### About the Project

**Chapter Journey** is the main Bible-reading channel and its companion website, guiding readers through Scripture one chapter at a time. It explores biblical events, people, context, and spiritual messages from a Coptic Orthodox perspective, helping connect the biblical text with everyday life.

**Spiritual Capsule** began as an independent WhatsApp channel and has become an important feature within Chapter Journey. It continues as a dedicated space for short spiritual and educational content alongside the channel's other sections. More than 440 followers are part of the journey.

### ✨ Explore the Content
- **Spiritual Capsules:** Browse a library of short-form content on faith and Christian life.
- **Multi-part Series:** Follow connected topics in order, from the series introduction through its individual parts.
- **Dedicated Sections:** Explore *Doctrine in a Hymn*, *Book in the Fifty Days*, *A Liturgical Moment*, *The Story Behind an Icon*, *Coptic Language*, *Ask and Learn*, and *A Message from Heaven*.
- **Bible and Faith:** Biblical stories and parables, selected verses and reflections, doctrinal topics, questions, and answers.
- **Rites and Tradition:** Content about prayer, the Divine Liturgy, sacraments, incense, icons, and fasting.
- **Church History:** People, Church figures, councils, and important moments in Church history.

### 🗓️ Church Occasions Archive
Explore Church feasts, fasts, and commemorations in one organized archive:
- Feasts of the Lord, Church fasts, and Holy Week.
- Saints’ commemorations, Coptic occasions, and other observances.
- Browse by category, chronological order, or name, and search for a specific occasion.
- View Coptic and Gregorian dates, with notes for movable feasts connected to the date of Easter.
- Find related Spiritual Capsules when content is available for an occasion.

### 🎮 Interactive Games and Challenges
- **Knowledge Challenge:** A 10-question round covering the Bible, Orthodox faith, and Church history.
- Each answer is followed by the correct answer, an explanation, and a reference.
- Scoring: 10 points for each correct answer, plus 3 bonus points for a correct answer submitted within the first 8 seconds.
- Every round offers a fresh set of questions, followed by a results summary showing correct and incorrect answers and the best score saved on the device.
- **“Who Am I?”:** Think of a biblical character or a figure from Orthodox Christian history. Answer the questions and let the game try to identify the person. Available responses include Yes, Probably, Probably Not, No, and I Don’t Know.

### 🎨 Appearance and Personalization
- **Four interface languages:** 🇪🇬 Arabic, 🇬🇧 English, 🇫🇷 French, and 🇩🇪 German, selectable from the language menu.
- **Dark and Light Modes:** Switch between the two appearances whenever you like.
- **Distinct Color Identity:** Dark mode uses a deep navy background with gold accents; light mode uses a bright background with purple and gold accents.
- **Favorites:** Save capsules to revisit later. Favorites are stored locally on your device.
- **Search:** Find content by capsule title or keywords.
- **Random Capsule:** Let the site choose a capsule when you are not sure what to read.
- **Simple Navigation:** Browse sections and move between views with navigation designed to respect right-to-left Arabic layout.
- **Responsive Design:** Built for comfortable use on phones, tablets, and desktop computers.

### 🤝 Follow and Get in Touch
The site links to the **Spiritual Capsule** WhatsApp channel for new capsules and the **Chapter Journey** channel for a daily Bible chapter. It also provides ways to send a question or feedback and get in touch.

> **Language note:** The interface is available in the four languages listed above. The published capsule and biblical content is primarily in Arabic; changing the interface language does not mean every content text has been fully translated.
