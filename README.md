### ✨ About the project
**Spiritual Capsule × Chapter Journey** brings together spiritual capsules, multi-part series, biblical and Church topics, an archive of feasts and fasts, and interactive Bible games — all from a Coptic Orthodox perspective.

### 🌍 What you'll find
- 📚 Spiritual capsules, themed sections, and connected series.
- 📖 Biblical stories, faith, doctrine, Church history, rites, and tradition.
- 🗓️ Church feasts, fasts, commemorations, and Coptic dates.
- 🎮 Bible knowledge challenges and the “Who Am I?” game.
- 🎨 Dark and light themes with distinct navy, gold, and purple accents.
- 🔎 Search, ❤️ favorites saved locally, and 🎲 random capsule discovery.
- 📱 Responsive experience on phones, tablets, and desktop.

> 🌐 The interface is available in four languages. Published capsule and biblical content is primarily Arabic; see each language page for the full language note.
